# Le discours indirect
Retour : [[00 - Sommaire TOEIC Grammar]] · Précédent : [[11 - La voix passive et les verbes causatifs]] · Suite : [[13 - Les propositions subordonnées relatives]]

Le discours indirect **rapporte** un discours direct. Il est introduit par des verbes comme *add, advise, agree, answer, apologize, ask, beg, command, decide, declare, deny, explain, inquire, object, order, plead, regret, remind, reply, request, require, say, suggest, tell, want to know, wonder*, conjugués dans des phrases commençant par *he said (that)*, *she thinks (that)*, *they wanted to know if*, *they asked whether*… Très fréquent en anglais commercial, ce point tombe souvent au TOEIC.

## 1. Du style direct au style indirect

Plusieurs éléments changent : le **sujet et ses déterminants**, le **temps**, les **repères temporels et spatiaux**. La proposition indépendante du discours direct devient une **proposition subordonnée complétive** : *She works on Sundays.* → *He said she worked on Sundays.*

### La concordance de personne

**Sujet identique** : il reste identique. *I've been busy all day.* → *I say that I've been busy all day.*

**Sujet différent** : il est modifié, ainsi que pronoms et adjectifs possessifs :

- *I've been busy all day.* → *She is saying **she** has been busy all day.*
- *My glasses are darker than yours.* → *He is saying that **his** glasses are darker than yours.*
- *Mine are safe.* → *He is saying that **his** are safe.*

### La concordance de temps

**Avec le présent** : si le verbe introductif est au présent, la subordonnée **garde le temps d'origine** : *I am positive that it would be a mistake to sell this mansion.*

**Avec un temps passé** : si le verbe introductif est au passé, la subordonnée se met au passé **à un degré supérieur** :

- *I missed the train.* → *I regretted I **had missed** my train.*
- *I couldn't take advantage of that discount.* → *I remembered that I **had not been able to** take advantage of that discount.*
- *The customs officers have been searching the truck.* → *I said that the customs officers **had been searching** the truck.*

### Le type de phrase

| Phrase directe | Discours indirect | Exemple |
|---|---|---|
| **Déclarative** | ordre des mots inchangé | *She works on Sundays.* → *He said she worked on Sundays.* |
| **Impérative** | proposition à l'**infinitif** | *Don't purchase this straw hat.* → *I advise you **not to purchase** this straw hat.* |
| **Interrogative** | ordre de la **déclarative** + *if / whether* ou mot interrogatif | *Would you do that for me?* → *I asked her **if she would do** that for me.* ; *Where did you buy these earrings?* → *I wanted to know **where she had bought** these earrings.* |

## 2. Récapitulatif

### Le sujet et le verbe

| Discours direct | Discours indirect |
|---|---|
| **présent** : *I know all his songs by heart.* | *I am saying (that) I **know** all his songs by heart.* |
| *I may know…* | *I am saying I **may** know…* |
| *I know…* | *He says (that) he **knows** all his songs by heart.* |
| **présent → prétérit** : *I know…* | *He said (that) he **knew** all his songs by heart.* |
| **prétérit** : *I must know…* | *I said (that) I **must / had to** know…* |
| **prétérit → past perfect** : *I knew…* | *He said (that) he **had known** all his songs by heart.* |
| **futur → conditionnel** : *I will know…* | *He said (that) he **would know** all his songs by heart.* |
| **impératif → infinitif complet** : *Learn his songs by heart!* | *She told me **to learn** his songs by heart.* |
| **présent continu → prétérit continu** : *When are you learning his songs?* | *She asked him when he **was learning** his songs by heart.* |

### La temporalité

Quand le verbe introductif est au passé, les repères temporels changent :

| Discours direct | Discours indirect |
|---|---|
| now | then |
| until now | until then |
| today | that day, that very day |
| next day | the following day, the day after, the next day |
| yesterday | the day before, the previous day |
| the day after tomorrow | two days after |
| the day before yesterday | two days before, two days earlier |
| last week | the week before |
| ago | before, earlier |

(Idem pour *week / month / year*.)

### Les repères spatiaux

| Discours direct | Discours indirect |
|---|---|
| here | there |
| this | that |
| these | those |

> [!warning] Pièges TOEIC
> - **Verbe introductif au prétérit** : le futur devient un conditionnel (*would*), le présent devient un prétérit, le prétérit devient un *past perfect*.
> - **Subordonnée de temps** (*as soon as he was released*) : jamais de futur ni de conditionnel ; c'est le prétérit qui s'impose, pas *will be* ni *would have been*.
> - Dans une complétive qui suit un verbe introductif au présent (*Joe Stevens wonders whether anybody …*), le futur reste au futur (*will ever get*).
> - Après *regretted that … the propeller …*, le fait est antérieur : *had broken*.

> [!tip] À retenir
> - Verbe introductif au **passé** → la subordonnée « recule » d'un temps (présent → prétérit, prétérit → past perfect, futur → conditionnel).
> - Interrogative rapportée : ordre de l'affirmative + *if / whether* ; impératif rapporté : infinitif complet.
> - *Now → then*, *today → that day*, *yesterday → the day before*, *here → there*.
